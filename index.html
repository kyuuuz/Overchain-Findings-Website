01 — What is it?

Overchain.io: Brief Intro

Overchain.io was created to solve the problems businesses face with traditional payment systems — especially when sending money across different countries.

The company is registered in Canada as Overchain.io (Canada) Limited, and has also expanded into London and Lithuania. From its launch, Overchain.io has focused on providing a fast and reliable way for firms to make and receive cryptocurrency payments.

Main Functions

Overchain.io is a cryptocurrency payment platform that helps businesses send, receive, and exchange a wide range of digital currencies from one place. The platform can automatically convert cryptocurrency into fiat currencies like USD, EUR, or GBP and send the funds directly to a business bank account — making international payments much simpler.

API Integration

Companies can connect Overchain.io directly to their own systems using its API.

Payment Links

Let customers pay with cryptocurrency from anywhere in the world via simple links.

Digital Invoices

Issue crypto invoices to clients globally, settled in crypto or fiat.

Payment Widgets

Embed crypto checkout directly into a website, app, or platform.

Auto-Conversion

Crypto automatically converted to USD, EUR, or GBP and paid to the bank.

Bulk Payments & OTC

Supports bulk payments and large over-the-counter (OTC) trades.

02 — Most Interesting Findings

My Three Most Interesting Findings

01

They work with the iGaming industry

Overchain.io actively serves iGaming — a high-velocity sector that traditional banks and payment providers often struggle or refuse to work with.

02

Settlements are done through Telegram

An unusual and standout operational detail — settlement communication is handled via Telegram, keeping the process fast and direct.

03

Crypto payouts funded directly from fiat

Businesses can fund crypto payouts straight from fiat accounts — no wallet or crypto holdings needed, removing the biggest barrier to adoption.

04 — Wrap-up

The Takeaway

Overchain.io's strengths

  • Broad crypto support settled straight to fiat bank accounts
  • OTC trading — neither BVNK nor BitPay offer it
  • No minimum volume — accessible where BVNK requires €500k/month
  • Serves hard-to-bank industries like iGaming and Forex
  • Crypto payouts funded directly from fiat — no wallet needed

Room to improve

  • Build a white-label wallet / widget SDK (learning from BVNK)
  • Expand coin support beyond ~8 major coins (learning from BitPay)
  • Add pre-built eCommerce plugins (Shopify, WooCommerce, Magento)
  • Grow brand recognition against established players
